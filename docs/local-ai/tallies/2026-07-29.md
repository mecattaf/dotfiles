# Local-AI tally — 2026-07-29 coordinator-only anchor

Cutoff: 2026-07-29, Europe/Paris. This supersedes the July 22 and July 26
topology and placement anchors, which were archival evidence only and were
removed from the tree on 2026-08-03; `docs/old/README.md` indexes them in Git
history.

## Accepted policy

- One AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 coordinator has 128 GiB unified RAM. Active
  llama.cpp model and external MTP weights therefore target Q8 fidelity:
  `UD-Q8_K_XL` or `Q8_0`.
- F16/BF16 vision projectors, BF16 speech models, tokenizers, and native
  FastFlowLM NPU2 snapshots remain explicit format exceptions.
- The coordinator enables XDNA2 and translated IOMMU mode. Managed local
  LLM/VLM endpoints enter through llama-swap; FastFlowLM remains direct,
  ad-hoc `flm run` with no persistent endpoint.
- The active catalog contains no distributed DS4 deployment. Historical
  benchmark and runbook evidence remains archival only.

## Active placement

The coordinator allowlist contains Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B, stock Qwen 3.6 27B,
Gemma 4 26B A4B IT, Fara 1.5 27B, the Qwen3-VL OCR pair, and the text and
multimodal Qwen3 embedders. FastFlowLM Gemma 4 E4B and GPT-OSS 20B remain
declared ad-hoc NPU models. VibeVoice ASR, VibeVoice Large, and their tokenizer
remain immutable coordinator artifacts.

The three uncensored deployments are explicitly retained as coordinator
catalog candidates only. They are absent from `services.local-models.allow`,
so this disposition does not authorize a download, model route, or service.

Exact identities, hashes, runtime arguments, and download totals live in
`lib/local-models.nix`, `docs/local-ai/model-roster.md`, and
`docs/local-ai/deployment-decisions-2026-07-29.md`.
