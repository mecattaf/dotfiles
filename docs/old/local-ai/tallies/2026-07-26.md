# Local-AI tally — 2026-07-26 Q8 and two-node anchor

Cutoff: 2026-07-26, Europe/Paris. This tally supersedes the active precision,
placement, and hardware choices in the 2026-07-22 normalization. Historical
catalog rows remain evidence only; host allowlists are the download boundary.

## Accepted policy

- Both AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 systems have 128 GiB unified RAM. Active
  llama.cpp model and external MTP weights therefore target Q8 fidelity:
  `UD-Q8_K_XL` or `Q8_0`.
- F16/BF16 vision projectors, BF16 speech models, tokenizers, and native
  FastFlowLM NPU2 snapshots are explicit format exceptions. They are not
  lower-bit llama.cpp selections.
- Both hosts enable the XDNA2 NPU and translated IOMMU mode. The worker gives
  up its former iGPU-only optimization for a uniform accelerator surface.
- Managed local LLM/VLM endpoints remain behind llama-swap. FastFlowLM NPU
  models are direct, ad-hoc `flm run` workloads with no persistent endpoint.
  Speech runtimes keep their modality-specific service boundary; Stable
  Diffusion remains outside the local-LLM route.

## Special managed models

- FastFlowLM owns `gemma4-it:e4b` and `gpt-oss:20b` runtime files on each host.
  Callers summon one model with `flm run <model>`; neither has a boot service or
  llama-swap peer.
- Voxtype owns `parakeet-unified-en-0.6b` on the coordinator. It is the largest
  registry choice compatible with the enabled streaming pipeline and is
  verified before service startup.
- VibeVoice ASR, VibeVoice Large, and their shared tokenizer are immutable
  coordinator payloads. Dedicated ROCm services remain future work.

## Monthly census correction

The monthly review now receives exact active weight filenames and quantization,
both hosts' current hardware policy, and the Q8 rule. It scans bounded
current-head inventories even when source pins do not move, adds accepted
catalog Hugging Face repositories to the metadata set, and ranks Q8/8-bit files
before the per-repository cap. A no-delta month completes the census without
creating a meaningless source-pin PR.

This tally does not authorize the monthly job to mutate the roster or download
models. Those remain explicit human changes to `lib/local-models.nix` and the
per-host allowlists.
